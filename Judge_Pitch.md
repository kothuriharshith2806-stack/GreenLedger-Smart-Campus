# 90-second judge pitch

Good morning. Our project is GreenLedger: Campus Resource Optimisation.

Campuses use electricity and water every day and generate waste, but these records are often separated. Without a common view, unusual changes can be missed and follow-up actions are hard to track.

GreenLedger combines all three resource categories in one laptop-based dashboard. It shows trends, compares each building with a baseline, flags readings that rise above a chosen threshold, and lets staff record an action, owner and status. Reports can be exported as CSV or text.

Our prototype does not need Arduino, sensors or paid cloud services. For this demonstration, the included dataset is synthetic sample data, clearly labelled as not measured. In a real campus pilot, we would connect authorised utility bills, meter logs and waste records, validate the data with facilities staff, and then measure whether corrective actions improve resource use.

GreenLedger helps turn scattered records into visible patterns and accountable follow-up. Thank you.
